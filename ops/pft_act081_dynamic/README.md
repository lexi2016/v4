## Preregistered method

- Root confirmation uses current valid `C>O`, `C>prior C`, and positive volume. First priority is a single ≥4% close advance and volume ≥2× the prior 20-session mean. Next is three consecutive bullish candles with rising closes and mean volume ≥1.5× the 20 observed sessions before that three-bar cluster. Otherwise a 10-session formation ending at the root requires net rise from the session before formation, more strictly bullish candles than bearish candles, positive bullish volume, and strictly bullish volume sum ≥1.25× the bearish sum. Dojis contribute to neither side. Root age 3–45 inclusive. A single root uses its own low; grouped roots use formation minimum low. The true bullish volume peak is fixed from strictly bullish bars in that formation.
- First available positive root starts the mother. A later opportunity cannot replace a live mother. The first post-root low strictly below the floor, an unobservable OHLC bar, a distribution flag or age >45 retires it. A same-day new positive root may start after retirement, but never triggers on that date. S1 distribution is volume ≥2× prior 20-day mean and high reaching the upper 20% of the prior 20-day high/low span, with upper wick ≥50% of the bar range or a bearish decline with body ≥1 prior ATR14. A declining bearish bar with volume ≥0.75× formation bullish peak and body ≥0.8 prior ATR14 also retires the mother. Daily qualification requires the P1 bullish-prior 3/4 false-breakout warning explicitly false over the last five observed sessions, indexed at the bear leg.
- W≥Y is required. A fresh upside crossing additionally allows at most 0.1 prior ATR14 day-over-day Y decline. Across the two consecutive five-session legs ending at D, accept a higher newer low with the newer high at most 1 prior ATR below its predecessor, or a platform with low difference ≤0.5 ATR and combined span ≤3 ATR. A simultaneous decline of both highs and lows by more than 0.5 ATR fails.
- On D, range ≤0.85× mean range over the ten sessions before D **or** ≤1.2× prior ATR14. Body ≤0.6× prior ATR14 and absolute close change ≤1.2× prior ATR14. D close above W by >0.8 ATR fails. D volume is positive and strictly below the fixed formation bullish peak. D close must be within 0.8 ATR of W or Y or 0–1 ATR above the unbroken root floor. ATR14, W and Y use frozen P1B definitions. W and Y are evaluated at D, all ATR thresholds use the D−1 value.
- CORE is all gates together. One P1B security clock allows strictly `gap>5` through episode changes. Accepted CORE FIRST is the first accepted CORE for its mother. STRICT is the exact same-day subset of accepted CORE FIRST where cumulative bullish volume from formation through D is positive and ≥1.5× bearish volume and confirmed P1 position is ≤35% above the 60-session low. No later refill of either FIRST or STRICT. Undefined values cannot qualify.
